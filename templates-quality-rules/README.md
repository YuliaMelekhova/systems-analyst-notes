# Templates and Quality Rules

The artifact templates and the rules that decide when a document is ready for someone else to read. BRD, SRS, ADR, API contracts and decision tables on one side. Naming, versioning, status values and readiness gates on the other.

Templates here ship with worked content rather than bracketed instructions. A section skeleton reading "describe the problem here" transfers no knowledge, and if the example cannot be written, the template is not understood well enough to publish.

The rules exist to move a completeness check from the reviewer to the author. They are not a quality bar for the content. A requirement can clear every gate and still be the wrong requirement, and what it cannot do is clear them and waste a reviewer's afternoon on a missing field.
